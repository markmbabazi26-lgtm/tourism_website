Good to know

A few helpful answers.

Have another question? Send an enquiry and discuss the details before making travel arrangements.

Are the prices shown on the website final?

No. The figures are indicative sample trip estimates, not confirmed quotations. The final cost depends on your dates, group size, itinerary, transport, accommodation, park fees and any permits. Request a written breakdown before paying.

Can I combine several national parks?

Yes, you can ask about a multi-destination itinerary. The route and timing will need to be planned around your available days, travel distances and the experiences you want.

How do gorilla and chimpanzee permits work?

Permit availability and prices can change. Ask about current availability for your preferred dates, and make sure permit arrangements are confirmed before treating a trek as booked.

Can the itinerary be customized?

Use the trip enquiry form to share your interests, dates and group size. You can discuss a route and trip plan that suits your needs before confirming anything.

How do I make an enquiry?

Complete the trip planner and open the prepared WhatsApp message, or use the WhatsApp and email details in the contact section. An enquiry is not a confirmed reservation.
